ISOMETRIC SPEC (2:1 dimetric). A ground tile is a diamond 32x16 px: top vertex (16,0), right (31,8), bottom (16,15), left (0,8); pixels outside the diamond are TRANSPARENT.
Build the diamond with poly([16,0, 32,8, 16,16, 0,8], key) or clip(); fill gradient/dither shading INSIDE it. Light from top-left: the top-left edge lighter, bottom-right edge darker. Tiles must seam: edge pixels of neighbouring diamonds meet without gaps; avoid dark outlines on tile borders (use subtle 1px tone shift instead).
Tall objects (trees, buildings, settlers) are drawn standing on a diamond footprint: sprite origin = bottom-centre of the footprint diamond; walls are two parallelograms (left face darker, right face mid, top/roof lightest), edges follow 2:1 slopes (2px across per 1px down). Footprint of a building of n x m tiles is a diamond of (n+m)*16 px wide.
Sprite size convention for this project: building b_iso_<type> width=(w+h)*16, height=(w+h)*8+extraHeight where extraHeight is roof/wall height (24-48px); the footprint diamond bottom vertex touches the image bottom-centre.
